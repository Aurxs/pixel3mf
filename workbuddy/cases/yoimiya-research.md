## Verified identity and visual facts

- Yoimiya is the fireworks artisan associated with Naganohara Fireworks in Inazuma; her element is Pyro and her weapon is a bow.
- Hair is light golden blonde, NOT red hair. It forms an asymmetrical high ponytail with upward/outward tufts, bangs, navy-blue loops, and red/orange and floral ornaments.
- Eyes are warm golden amber, with a cheerful, lively expression.
- Her asymmetrical festival outfit combines orange/red kimono fabric, dark navy accents, white bandage wrapping across the upper chest, and a small dark choker with a gold detail. One shoulder is exposed.
- A large red braided rope ornament forms bold loops behind her upper body. These rope loops and the high ponytail are useful recognizable silhouette features.
