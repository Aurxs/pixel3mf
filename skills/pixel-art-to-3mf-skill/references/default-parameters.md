# Default Parameters

Use these defaults unless the user explicitly overrides them.

## Composition
- Force a square composition.
- Keep the subject horizontally centered and shift it upward by about 1–2 logical pixels.
- Default to a natural upper-body portrait: head, shoulders, and upper chest only, without extending below the chest.
- Do not add special restrictions on hands, arms, or joints; allow the pose to remain natural within the crop.
- Make the head, hair, face, and identifying accessories the dominant visual mass.
- Default to a readable three-quarter head turn with both eyes visible; use full frontal or full profile only when explicitly requested.
- Preserve three-quarter depth through asymmetric cheek, jaw, nose, hair, and shoulder shapes; do not turn the pose frontal just to align the eyes.
- Build both eyes from the same vertical pixel template: identical top/bottom rows and identical iris/pupil row counts, with aligned centers and matching gaze. Allow the far eye to be at most one logical cell narrower, never shorter. Other elements may overlap sclera but must not shorten iris/pupil height or move the eye anchors.
- Keep the neck short and naturally broad at the base as it joins the collar and shoulders. Unless the character design requires otherwise, use a visible neck opening roughly one-quarter to one-third of the lower-face width and start the collar within a few logical rows below the jaw.
- Keep a small top margin and a larger bottom margin, including at least one background row below the subject's bottom outline.
- Preserve transparent background when possible.

## Output Folder
- Root output directory: `output/`
- Per-run subfolder: `output/<timestamp>_<slug>/`

## Lumina Defaults
- Size goal: 55 mm × 55 mm square
- Backing thickness: 1.2 mm
- Structure mode: Double-sided
- Hanging loop: disabled
- LUT preset target: `Bambulab&PLA&4色&RYBW&红-蓝-黄-白.npy`
- Modeling mode: pixel / pixel-art mode
- Quantize colors: 256
- Batch conversion: preferred
- Intermediate outputs: always retain

## Background Removal
Use a two-stage strategy:
1. segmentation / background-removal model if available
2. near-white to transparent fallback

## Pixel Refinement
- Use Perfect Pixel with auto grid detection by default.
- Use auto-detection once as a source-density acceptance diagnostic before conversion: prefer approximately 44–60 detected cells per axis and regenerate when either axis exceeds 60.
- Preserve the automatically detected grid; do not force 24 × 24 or 55 × 55 during refinement.
- Record the actual nominal pixel pitch from the detected output width and the 55 mm physical target.
- If automatic detection fails, regenerate a clearer coarse-pixel source rather than selecting a fallback grid.
- After refinement, if width != height, pad with transparency to square.
- Save an enlarged nearest-neighbor preview.

## Source Image Generation
- Request an internal 24 × 24 logical canvas as a deliberate preventive undershoot, followed only by nearest-neighbor enlargement. Do not expect the measured output grid to equal 24 × 24.
- Match the visible block scale and low information density of `reference-coarse-density-a.png` and `reference-coarse-density-b.png`; do not copy their subject matter.
- Request a natural upper-body portrait that does not extend below the chest, without imposing special hand or joint constraints.
- Align every contour and color boundary to the same logical grid, and forbid features or color regions smaller than one logical cell.
- Use large uniform blocks, a one-logical-pixel pure-black exterior outline, stair-stepped contours, and very little interior detail.
- Fully enclose the subject in the outline. Make the bottom-most occupied row a continuous black baseline and keep at least one background row below it.
- Use roughly 8–12 discrete flat colors; avoid individual hair strands, micro-texture, dithering, smooth gradients, anti-aliasing, and illustration-style highlights.
- A high-resolution generated bitmap should look like a nearest-neighbor enlargement of one uniform coarse logical grid.
- Inspect the result rather than trusting the requested dimensions. Regenerate at the image-generation stage when the design is still too detailed; do not correct logical density later.

## 3MF Project Settings
- Preserve the 3MF settings generated by Lumina.
- Do not rewrite the printer model, layer heights, first layer, bed geometry, or machine G-code.
- Let the user inspect and adjust printer settings later in Bambu Studio.
