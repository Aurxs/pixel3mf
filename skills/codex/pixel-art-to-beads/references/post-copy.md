# Concise bead-post copy

Use this template for future requested bead-post drafts. Keep the labels and prose Chinese. Use the reviewed Chinese character name; never invent a translation or personal reaction. Do not require an opening anecdote, personality hook, personal impression, emotional ending or engagement question. Do not change historical works or posts.

## Title

`角色名拼豆图纸｜附镜像`

Replace only `角色名` with the verified name. This is the post title; chart titles retain their existing rules.

## Body

For an original/mirror pair:

```text
角色：{中文角色名}
尺寸：{宽}×{高}格
色数：{实际色数}色
豆数：{实际豆数}颗
图序：{按本次实际上传顺序填写}
色卡：{实际使用色卡名称}
```

When the delivery also includes a 52-board version:

```text
角色：{中文角色名}
原尺寸：{原向宽}×{原向高}格｜{原向色数}色｜{原向豆数}颗
52板：{该版实际宽}×{该版实际高}格｜{该版色数}色｜{该版豆数}颗
图序：{按本次实际上传顺序填写}
色卡：{实际使用色卡名称；不同版本使用不同色卡时分别注明}
```

Read each delivered variant's saved `图纸数据.json` statistics and palette record; verify against the exported charts. Use the trimmed original dimensions and each 52-board chart's actual grid dimensions. “52板” describes that version; do not assume its dimensions, colors or bead total equal the original. Count only colors actually used and occupied bead cells. Each mirror shares its corresponding original's counts; do not double the total. Do not include a withdrawn or unaccepted variant. If statistics are missing, inspect the saved cells and derive them or report the missing field instead of guessing.

Match `图序` to the exact images being delivered: include the cover only if present, then identify every original and mirror shown. For example, a five-image upload may read `1封面，2原尺寸，3原尺寸镜像，4五十二板，5五十二板镜像`; a two-image upload has only its original and mirror. Never assume a fixed image count when a cover or variant is absent. Preserve the palette's verified name/model identifier instead of inventing a Chinese brand name; any factual identifiers do not justify foreign-language prose.
